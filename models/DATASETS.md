# Iris demo attribution

Fisher, R. (1936). Iris. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C56C76 . Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

Training uses scikit-learn's copy. Measurements and labels were split into train/test sets, standardized and used to train a classifier. The learned normalization and parameters are embedded in iris_classifier.pte. Three example measurements are provided as presets. No downloaded third-party model weights are used.
